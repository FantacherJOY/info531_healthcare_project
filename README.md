### Card Overview

- **Card Definition:** A — Assessment, Prevention, and Management of Pain.  
- **Purpose:** To observe pain level, assign a pain score, and document significant pain assessment.

### Assessment Instruments

- **Critical Care Pain Observation Tool (CPOT)** is a four-item scale—vocalization, body movements, facial expression, and muscle tension—each scored from 0 to 2.  
- **Alternative tools:** Numeric Pain Rating Scale (NRS) and Behavioral Pain Scale (BPS).

### Compliance Checks

- Significant pain is defined as **CPOT > 2**, or **BPS > 5**, or **NRS > 3**.  
- Conduct pain assessments at least **six times** within 24 hours to monitor compliance.
